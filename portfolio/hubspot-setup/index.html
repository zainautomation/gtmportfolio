HubSpot, built from zero into a real revenue engine.

A full HubSpot implementation from the ground up: clean data mapping, inbound forms wired to enrichment, automatic lead routing, a real contact-to-deal pipeline, calendar sync, lifecycle workflows, and marketing email built on top of it.

HubSpot Lead routing Marketing ops RevOps systems

The live HubSpot instance, built and running

The problem

There was no real system behind the CRM, just a database. Leads came in from different sources with inconsistent fields, nobody owned routing, and reps were manually moving records between contacts, leads, and deals. Marketing emails and calendar bookings sat outside the CRM entirely, so none of it rolled up into one pipeline.

The fix

A HubSpot instance built from scratch with a single source of truth: standardized data mapping across every object, inbound forms that feed straight into enrichment and routing, and a clean conversion path from contact to lead to deal. Calendar bookings, workflows, and marketing emails are wired into the same system instead of running alongside it.

How it works

  • 1
    Data mappingEvery contact, company, and deal property standardized so records stay clean and comparable across sources.
  • 2
    Inbound formsWebsite and landing page forms feed submissions directly into HubSpot with no manual entry.
  • 3
    Lead enrichmentIncoming leads are automatically enriched with firmographic and contact data on arrival.
  • 4
    Lead routingLeads are assigned to the right owner automatically based on defined criteria, no manual triage.
  • 5
    Contact → lead → deal conversionA defined path moves qualified records from contact to lead to deal without losing history.
  • 6
    Calendar syncMeeting bookings sync directly to rep calendars and log against the right record.
  • 7
    WorkflowsLifecycle and nurture automation runs on top of the same clean data, not a separate tool.
  • 8
    Marketing emailsCampaigns run natively in HubSpot, targeted off the same lists and lifecycle stages as sales.

End result

One HubSpot instance running lead management, routing, conversion, calendars, workflows, and marketing email off the same clean data, currently tracking over 87,000 lead/infos with zero manual handoffs between systems.

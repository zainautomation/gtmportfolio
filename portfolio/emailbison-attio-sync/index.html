EmailBison to Attio, reply sync on autopilot.

An n8n workflow that watches every outbound email campaign for replies and writes them straight into the CRM, so no inbound lead or conversation ever sits unlogged.

n8n · automation EmailBison Attio CRM RevOps systems

The live workflow, built and running in n8n

The problem

Outbound campaigns were running through EmailBison, but every reply arrived in an inbox, not a CRM record. Reps were manually copying prospect replies into Attio, which meant inconsistent logging, lost context on who'd actually responded, and no reliable view of reply activity across campaigns.

The fix

A webhook-triggered n8n workflow sits between EmailBison and Attio. The moment a prospect replies, n8n receives the payload, maps the fields it needs, and writes the record straight into the CRM without anyone touching it.

How it works

  • 1
    Webhook triggerEmailBison detects an incoming reply and fires a webhook to n8n.
  • 2
    Data processingn8n receives the payload and maps it to the fields Attio expects.
  • 3
    Company creationA company record is created in Attio from the available account information.
  • 4
    Contact creationA contact record is created and linked to that company.
  • 5
    Email activity syncThe reply content and metadata are synced to the contact's email activity timeline in Attio.

End result

Every inbound reply from an EmailBison campaign is automatically captured in Attio, with the matching company, contact, and email activity already in place. Zero manual data entry, and a CRM record centralized the moment a prospect replies.
